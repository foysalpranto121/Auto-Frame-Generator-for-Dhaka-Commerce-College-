GOOD SOURCE PHOTO

Face forward, decent light

Square or portrait shots fit the circle best. A photo taken against a window or outdoors beats a dim indoor selfie every time.

MIND THE WAVE

Keep your chin above the band

The teal wave carrying the batch name covers the bottom of the circle. Drag the picture up a little so your chin clears that line.

WHICH SIZE

Facebook DP for profiles

Facebook and WhatsApp crop profile pictures to a circle, so Facebook DP scales the frame until its ring fills that circle exactly. Story adds the date and venue underneath for status posts.
